Why we exist

Our mission.

Our mission is to make HVAC tools and supplies easy to explore. We organize service tools, installation supplies, and maintenance essentials into a clear catalog so you can find what your next project needs.

Where we started

Our brand story.

JNG Supply began as a storefront concept inspired by hands-on HVAC work in Charlotte, North Carolina. From troubleshooting a system to finishing an installation, having the right supplies makes a difference.

That idea shaped our collection: practical tools and everyday essentials for technicians, students learning the trade, and homeowners taking on maintenance projects.
